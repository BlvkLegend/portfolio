About

Interfaces that work

I work across interface design and frontend development, shaping business websites, product systems and landing pages into structured digital experiences that move from intent to working product with clarity and control.

My Computer Science degree (CGPA 4.38) and over 3+ years of commercial work shape a disciplined approach to building, focused on structure, usability and flow, treating interfaces as connected systems rather than isolated screens.

  • Independent delivery Brief to launch, solo, across multiple industries.
